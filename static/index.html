      The honest limits

      • Can be removed: data brokers, people-search sites, and Google search results — they must answer a legal erasure request.
      • Cannot be removed: a breach dump already copied to Telegram, dark-web forums or torrents. No tool on earth can delete every copy. Anyone promising that is lying.
      • So the real defence is both: remove what's removable, and change any password that appears in a breach — everywhere you reused it.
